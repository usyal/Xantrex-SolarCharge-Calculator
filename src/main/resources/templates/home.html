Purpose of the Application

The application helps size photovoltaic module arrays to ensure they are compatible with a specific charge controller’s electrical limits and performance constraints.
It calculates acceptable series/parallel configurations of solar panels based on electrical and environmental conditions.

How The Application Works

Required Inputs

  • Maximum Power of a single panel (Pmax) in Watts
  • Open Circuit Voltage of a single panel (Voc) in Volts
  • Maximum Power Voltage of a single panel (Vmp) in Volts
  • Short Circuit Charge of a single panel (Isc) in Amps
  • Maximum Power Current of a single panel (Imp) in Amps
  • Number of Panels in Series Ns and Parallel NP
  • System Voltage Vnom of Solar Array & Battery Type used in Solar Array

Solar Array Computational Formulas

Total Array Power = Pmax * Ns * Np

Open Circuit Voltage = Voc * Ns * temp. coefficient

Max Current Charge = Total Array Power / Vnom

Max Power Voltage = Vmp * Ns

Max Power Current = Imp * Np

*Default temp. coefficient is set to 1
